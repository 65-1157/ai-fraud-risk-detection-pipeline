\# Executive Summary



This project implements an end-to-end fraud risk detection pipeline using synthetic financial transaction data.



The pipeline covers:



\- data generation;

\- data cleaning;

\- PySpark feature engineering;

\- supervised fraud classification;

\- unsupervised anomaly detection;

\- final risk scoring;

\- local GenAI-style explanations;

\- monitoring;

\- SQL validation queries;

\- unit tests.



The final output is a transaction-level risk table with:



\- fraud probability;

\- anomaly score;

\- rule-based score;

\- final risk score;

\- risk level;

\- recommended action;

\- business-readable explanation.



The project is local-first and does not require paid cloud, Databricks, private banking data, or paid GenAI APIs.



It is designed to demonstrate practical skills for Data Science, Risk Analytics, Fraud Analytics, Machine Learning Engineering, and AI Engineering interviews.
